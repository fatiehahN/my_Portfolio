
# 🛡️ CyberWira 3D Defender
### An Interactive 3D Cybersecurity Awareness AdverGame

**CyberWira 3D Defender** is a 3D cybersecurity awareness AdverGame developed as a Final Year Project (FYP). It aims to promote cybersecurity awareness through interactive gameplay and simulated real-world cybersecurity scenarios.

The project focuses on educating users about common online threats, encouraging safer digital behaviour, and improving cybersecurity decision-making through immersive learning experiences.

CyberWira is designed as an interactive educational platform for schools, ICT and STEM clubs, and external cybersecurity organizations to support cybersecurity awareness programmes, workshops, and learning activities.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

- Traditional cybersecurity awareness methods, such as posters, talks, and text-based materials, are often passive and may fail to engage users effectively.
- Existing cybersecurity awareness games commonly rely on simple 2D environments or quiz-based approaches, which may lack realistic and immersive learning experiences.
- Many existing systems provide limited feedback and progress tracking, making it difficult for users to identify their cybersecurity weaknesses and improve their knowledge.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

CyberWira addresses these challenges through an interactive 3D AdverGame that combines cybersecurity education with engaging gameplay.

The system provides:

- **Interactive 3D Learning Environment:** Simulated cybersecurity scenarios that allow players to experience common online threats.
- **Three Cybersecurity Challenges:** Game levels focusing on Password Security, Phishing Detection, and Online Scam Prevention.
- **Immediate Feedback:** Provides explanations and feedback based on players' decisions and mistakes.
- **In-Game Analysis:** Tracks and evaluates player performance and behavioural patterns to identify cybersecurity weaknesses.
- **Performance Dashboard:** Allows players to review their individual performance, while teachers and organizers can monitor multiple players.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

1. To design a 3D interactive environment based on the AdverGame concept for cybersecurity awareness.
2. To develop three immersive game levels focusing on Password Security, Phishing Attacks, and Online Scams.
3. To implement an In-Game Analysis system to track and evaluate users' behavioural patterns and gameplay performance.

## 🎮 Game Levels

### 🔐 Level 1: Password Security
Educates players about strong password creation, password reuse risks, and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

### 📧 Level 2: Phishing Detection
Challenges players to identify suspicious emails, recognize phishing indicators, and distinguish legitimate messages from malicious attempts.

### 🛒 Level 3: Online Scam Prevention
Simulates online shopping situations where players must evaluate sellers, inspect product information, identify suspicious reviews, and avoid fraudulent payment requests.

## 📊 Performance Analytics System

CyberWira incorporates an In-Game Analysis system to evaluate player performance and cybersecurity decision-making throughout the challenges.

### Player Performance Dashboard

- Individual scores and level progress.
- Stars and achievement tracking.
- Cybersecurity strengths and weaknesses.
- Mistake summaries and improvement suggestions.
- Gameplay performance insights.

### Teacher / Organizer Analytics Dashboard

- Overview of registered players.
- Player performance and level completion.
- Overall scores and performance comparisons.
- Common mistakes and cybersecurity weaknesses.
- Behavioural analysis and decision-making insights.

This feature helps teachers and organizers evaluate player performance and identify cybersecurity topics that may require further attention.
